From Haneda Airport

Take the Keikyu Line to Shinbashi, transfer to Ginza Line, then Hanzomon Line to Kiyosumi-Shirakawa. Exit from B2.

View Step-by-Step

From Narita Airport

Take the Keisei Skyliner to Nippori, transfer to Yamanote Line to Shinbashi, then follow the Haneda route.

View Step-by-Step

From Tokyo Station

A quick journey from Tokyo's central hub to our hotel via the efficient Tokyo Metro system.

  • 🚉 Take Marunouchi Line to Otemachi Station
  • 🔄 Transfer to Hanzomon Line
  • 🚇 Take Hanzomon Line to Kiyosumi-Shirakawa
  • ⏱️ Total journey: 15-20 minutes
  • 🚶 Exit from B2 and walk 2 minutes

🎫 Ticket Tips

• IC cards (Suica/Pasmo) accepted
• Cost: Around ¥200
• Platform signs in English available

🗺️ Walking Route from B2 Exit

Follow the blue dotted path for the most direct route to our entrance.

  1. Exit Kiyosumi-Shirakawa Station via B2 exit
  2. Head east along the main street
  3. Pass the FamilyMart on your right
  4. Our building entrance is just ahead
  5. Look for the private driveway entrance

🚶 The Private Walkway

This private walkway connects both streets and leads to our building entrance.

  • 🔄 Makes a turn at the halfway point
  • 🏘️ Shared with neighboring properties
  • 🌙 Well-lit at night for safety
  • 🚪 White building entrance at the end

The walkway is a shared space. Please keep noise to a minimum, especially during evening and early morning hours.

🚭 No Smoking Policy

Please be aware of our strict no-smoking policy and local regulations.

  • 🏢 Entire building is smoke-free (all floors)
  • 🔝 Rooftop area is also strictly no-smoking
  • 🏙️ Tokyo law prohibits smoking on streets in this area
  • 💰 Street smoking violations can result in fines
  • ⚠️ Building violations may affect your stay

⛔ Important Warning

Smoking is NOT allowed anywhere in the building including rooms, hallways, stairwells, and rooftop. Please respect this policy to ensure everyone's comfort and safety.
